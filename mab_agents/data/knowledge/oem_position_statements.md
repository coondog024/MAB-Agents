# OEM collision position statements: what each maker requires

Compiled 2026-10-21. Every PDF below was fetched and its issuer and date
confirmed by the research run that produced this page; the live documents are
listed in `sources.toml` so `mab kb fetch` pulls the current versions into
this folder. Position statements are the manufacturer's written requirements;
quote them by name and date on supplements and keep the PDF in the RO file.
OEM1Stop (oem1stop.com) and the I-CAR RTS position-statement hub
(rts.i-car.com/collision-repair-news/oem-position-statements.html) are the two
indexes; RTS mirrors most PDFs under rts.i-car.com/images/pdf/oem-info/.

## Scanning: who requires pre- and post-repair scans

| OEM | Requirement | Document |
|---|---|---|
| GM (Chevrolet, Buick, GMC, Cadillac) | Pre-scan during the estimate and post-scan after all repairs on every vehicle assessed for collision damage; GM Techline (GDS2/Tech2Win) is the endorsed method; any battery interruption requires retest | Pre & Post Diagnostic Scanning REQUIRED, 2026-03-09, gmparts.com |
| Ford / Lincoln | Pre- and post-repair scans required on all 1996+ vehicles with more than minor cosmetic damage; IDS (1996-2018) or FDRS (2018+) | Pre- and Post-Diagnostic Scanning REQUIRED, 2025-10-01; ADAS Integrity and Repair Technical Imperatives, 2026-05-01 (makes all listed standards mandatory) |
| Honda / Acura | All OBD-II vehicles in a collision must get a preliminary scan during estimating and a post-repair scan with no DTCs; any electrical disconnection or body-part replacement always requires a post-repair scan; i-HDS All DTC Check is the only scan Honda recognises | Post-Collision Diagnostic Scan and Calibration Requirements, July 2025 v6 |
| Stellantis (Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep, Ram, Fiat, Alfa) | wiTECH scan necessary before and after collision repair including minor and no-deployment events and any disassembly, trim or glass removal; multi-stage airbags must be checked for all squibs fired; remote wiTECH (asTech) acceptable | Scan Tool Usage, 2025-12-01 |
| Nissan / Infiniti | Pre- and post-repair scan required on all 2008+ vehicles (recommended older), CONSULT only; calibrations with CONSULT and approved targets | NPSB/25-946, 2025-11-01 |
| Toyota / Lexus | "Health Check" scan necessary before and after every collision repair (Techstream); CRIB 191, inside Toyota TIS (subscription) | I-CAR RTS crn-429 summary |
| Subaru | Pre-repair scan required, post-repair scan required (SSM3/SSM4); EyeSight inspection/calibration after collision or body repair | Pre- and Post-Scanning, Aug 2020 |
| Hyundai (and Genesis) | Pre- and post-repair scan **required** since the June 2025 revision (was "recommended") | Pre-Repair and Post-Repair System Scanning, June 2025 |
| Kia | Pre- and post-repair scans "imperative", "an essential task", not an option | Kia Pre and Post Diagnostic Scanning, June 2018 (kiatechinfo.snapon.com) |
| Mazda | Pre- and post-repair scan with M-MDS essential regardless of warning lights; post-scan required | Pre- and Post-Repair Diagnostic Scanning, 2025-10-31 (was "recommends", 2018) |
| Volvo | Pre-repair scan required before any work, post-repair scan required for any work; VIDA; battery maintainer during sessions | Scanning and Diagnostics, 2024 revision |
| Mercedes-Benz | Pre-repair scan "highly recommended", post-repair scan "necessary" (XENTRY) | Diagnostic Repairs Following a Collision |
| BMW / MINI | Modules, sensors and cameras must be evaluated with ISTA before and after repair | SIB 41 05 19 (subscription site; I-CAR crn-1356 summary) |
| VW / Audi | No standalone scanning statement; scan requirements are inside erWin repair procedures | I-CAR crn-2405/2406 |
| Tesla | No classic statement; procedures require Service Mode / Toolbox diagnostics (event-based alerts, not generic DTCs) | I-CAR crn-2117; service.tesla.com Body Repair Procedures |
| Rivian | Pre- and post-repair scans with RiDE required, by Rivian Certified Technicians | RCI-98-22-007-2, 2026-04-28 |

Practical rule: write the pre-repair scan on the initial estimate and the
post-repair scan plus any calibration as separate lines; attach both reports to
the RO; hand the post-scan and calibration report to the customer at delivery.

## Parts: aftermarket, recycled, reconditioned

- **GM** "strictly prohibits" non-GM-approved salvage, recycled or aftermarket parts and non-GM reconditioned parts in collision, structural or system-dependent repairs; bumper fascias on ADAS vehicles must be new GM; non-OE windshields prohibited (statements dated 2026-03 to 2026-09).
- **Ford** validates only Ford OE parts; recycled, salvaged, aftermarket and reconditioned panels, structural members, bumper reinforcements, wheels, restraints, fasteners, cameras and sensors are not validated (2025-10-01); ADAS sensors from secondary sources not approved (2026-05-01).
- **Honda** does not support aftermarket or reverse-engineered structural parts or re-use of structural parts from another vehicle (2010); non-OE windshields, bumpers and grilles can defeat Honda Sensing (2018); non-repairable zones on bumper covers near the rear radar (Sept 2024).
- **Stellantis** does not approve aftermarket, recycled, salvaged or reconditioned parts; panels installed in their entirety, sectioning only where the body repair manual gives a procedure (2025-12-01).
- **Nissan** does not approve aftermarket, grey-market or imitation parts (NPSB-16-594); salvaged, aftermarket or secondary-market ADAS components not approved (NPSB/26-971, 2026-01-01).
- **Subaru** does not approve recycled, salvaged, aftermarket or reconditioned parts (Aug 2021); strongly recommends genuine windshield glass for EyeSight.
- **Hyundai / Kia** do not recommend aftermarket, imitation, recycled or remanufactured collision parts; Kia endorses only Kia-authorised remanufactured components.
- **Mazda** does not authorise aftermarket or salvaged parts (recommendation strength, 2024-12-06).
- **VW / Audi** do not support aftermarket, reverse-engineered or remanufactured structural parts or parts salvaged from another vehicle.
- **Mercedes-Benz** does not approve aftermarket or third-party parts or salvaged components; collision-damaged fasteners replaced with genuine.
- **Volvo** aftermarket, salvage and reconditioned parts not approved; windshields and panoramic roof glass only Volvo genuine.
- **Toyota** CRIB 157 (2007): no used, salvage or imitation parts, specifically SRS components.
- **Tesla / Rivian** do not support third-party, used or recycled parts (Rivian also excludes Opt-OE and externally remanufactured).

Use with the Maryland §14-2302 disclosure: when a carrier estimate specifies
aftermarket crash parts on one of these makes, cite the statement on the
supplement and give the customer the OEM-upgrade difference in writing.

## Restraints and one-time-use parts

- Deployed pretensioners are single-use; after any deployment inspect every belt, retractor and buckle (Nissan NPSB-16-601; Volvo requires replacing the steering wheel, pretensioners and buckles on occupied seats; Ford points to the Workshop Manual belt inspection after any collision).
- Salvaged or used airbag components: not approved by GM (2026-06-23, "strictly prohibits"), Honda (2007), Ford, Nissan, Subaru, Kia, VW.
- One-time-use parts and fasteners must be replaced when removed: Stellantis OTUP statement (2025-12-01: clips, torque-to-yield bolts, adhesive-backed weatherstrips), Nissan NPSB/18-420, Ford structural fasteners and adhesives (self-piercing, blind, solid and flow-drill rivets must be Ford-original), Tesla and Rivian fastener guidelines.
- Occupant classification and steering-column inspection after a frontal impact appear in several makers' procedures (Stellantis Steering Systems statement; the shop's not-included catalogue carries both checks).

## Structure, sectioning, heat, welders

- Clip repair (sectioning two vehicles together) not approved: GM (2026), Ford, Hyundai (2018), VW, Volvo.
- Sectioning only per the body repair manual: Honda (2007), Stellantis, Nissan (factory seams), Ford (exterior skin only where procedures allow), Subaru (certified centre recommended).
- Use of heat on structural and non-structural components: Stellantis and Hyundai statements (Jan 2025) restrict it; follow the body repair manual.
- Approved inverter resistance spot welders only: VW; weld-through primer and corrosion protection appear in every maker's procedures and in the shop's catalogue.

## Wheels

Every maker that has a statement says the same thing: no welding, bending,
straightening, re-machining or adding material; cosmetic refinishing only; a
cracked or bent-flange wheel is replaced. GM (2026-03-09, with Zyglo crack
check), Ford (2025-10-01), Honda (2007), Toyota CRIB 154, Stellantis
(2025-12-01), Nissan NPSB-16-595, Subaru (2015), Hyundai (2018),
Mercedes-Benz, Volvo, Rivian. Do not accept a carrier's "reconditioned wheel"
line on these makes without the customer's informed written consent.

## Glass, refinish and ADAS zones

- OEM glass: GM prohibits non-OE windshields (2026); Ford, Volvo, Stellantis (Mopar), Subaru (EyeSight), Hyundai (2023) and Rivian (signed acknowledgement) state the same; every forward camera needs calibration after glass work.
- Clearcoat blending not approved: Ford (edges fall below 2-mil minimum), Hyundai (2018), Nissan NPSB/18-417, Volvo. Blend the full panel.
- Bumper covers with radar: Honda defines non-repairable zones around the rear BSI radar (Sept 2024); Stellantis limits refinish film build to about 2.5-4 mils (never over 12 mils or three topcoats), no filler within 25 cm of the sensor, no bracket repair, BSM calibration plus post-scan required (2025-12-01); Ford bans fillers, tape, hot staples and plastic welding on ADAS fascias; GM requires new GM fascias on ADAS vehicles (2026-09-18); Hyundai bumpers with corner radar statement (Jan 2025); Nissan ARIYA bumper refinishing requirements (2026).
