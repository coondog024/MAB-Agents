# Day one at the front desk with MAB

You do not need to know collision repair to run the front desk with this. You
need a browser, a phone, and this page.

## Open the app

On the front-desk PC, in a terminal, once:

```bash
cd MAB-Agents
mab serve
```

It prints a link that ends in `?token=...`. Open **that exact link** and
bookmark it; it signs the browser in for a year. Keep the tab open all day. (A
tablet on the shop Wi-Fi uses the same kind of link; the manager sets it up and
it should stay on the shop's private network.)

## Your morning, in order

1. **Today tab.** The top-left list is every customer who needs a call or a text
   today and why. Work it top down; red ones first. Click **Draft status text**,
   read it, send it from the shop phone, click **Mark sent**. If the card says the
   promised date has passed, the button is **Delay text (new date)** instead: get
   the new date from production first, then type the reason in plain words. That
   moves the target date on the RO as well.
2. Right side: cars **waiting on approval** (nudge the adjuster if older than a
   day), **overdue** promises (send a delay text; ask production for the new date
   first), and how many **rentals** are running.
3. Glance at the **production board**. You are not managing it, but you should
   know where each car is when a customer asks.

## When the phone rings

- **A new customer.** Start with the person and the car: "Is everyone OK? Is the
  car drivable? Where is it now?" Insurance questions come after. Never open
  with "Have you filed a claim yet?"
- **Customer asking about their car.** Customer tab → type their phone number →
  open the RO. The page tells you the stage in plain words, what they owe at
  pickup, how many rental days they have used, and whether parts are late. The
  **Status text** button gives you a message you can read aloud or send; every
  text ends with when they will hear from us next.
- **"What do I say when…"** Scripts tab. Every common call has an *Ask / Say /
  Never / Then* card: new claim, status call, angry about a delay, deductible,
  adjuster calling, tow arriving, rental, after-hours pickup, "my insurer says I
  must use their shop", customer-pay estimate.
- **Something the scripts don't cover.** Ask the fleet tab. Type the situation
  the way you'd tell a coworker. It answers from the shop's own playbook and
  tells you when to get the manager.

## New car coming in

Check-in tab. Fill the form while they're on the phone (name, mobile, year,
make, model, what happened, insurer and claim number, deductible, rental company
and how many rental days the policy covers). The covered days matter: the Today
list warns you before a rental runs out. Click **Create RO**. You get the RO number, the photo list for when the car arrives, and a
**Print the repair authorization** button. Print it, have the customer read the
"Customer's Rights" box and sign above it, and keep the signed copy with the RO. If the
car came in on a hook or after hours, read those rights to the customer by phone and fill
in the box at the bottom of the form (who you told, date, time, your signature). The
check-in and delivery checklists are on the same page.

Every RO page has a **Paperwork** row. The buttons tell you which forms this job needs
and when: the aftermarket parts sheet before parts are ordered (the customer sees which
parts are not factory parts and signs), the additional-repairs consent when a supplement
or a customer-pay item is added, and the invoice addendum at pickup. Each opens a print
view in a new tab.

## Money

Never quote a balance from memory. Open the RO: the **If they ask what they owe,
say** line gives you the words. On insurance jobs it is the deductible plus any
items the insurer declined that are already on the RO; the final insurer payment
can still change things, which is why the line says we'll tell them before
pickup. If it says the deductible isn't on file yet, ask the adjuster or the
customer and add it before anyone is quoted a number. We do not waive
deductibles. Card, cash or check.

At pickup, print the **invoice addendum** from the Paperwork row: it lists any used or
rebuilt parts, has the box for whether the customer took their old parts, shows the
balance, and repeats the required notices. The customer signs it and takes a copy.

## Parts questions

Parts tab. **Price a part** turns a list price or a vendor quote into what we
bill. **Compare quotes** ranks vendors by total cost to the job including the
days the customer sits in a rental. **Source a part online** sends the Parts
agent to look up a part number across dealer sites, RockAuto, Amazon and eBay
and come back with a shortlist. Hand anything you'd actually order to the parts
person or the manager.

## Get the manager when

- The customer asks for the manager.
- An adjuster says no to a scan, a calibration or a seat belt.
- Anyone says the words "total loss", "lawyer", "insurance commissioner" or "BBB" (the Scripts tab has a total-loss card for the first conversation).
- A supplement has sat with the adjuster for three days.
- Anything about injury, a spill, or damage to a customer's car in our care.

## What the app will never do

Send a message for you, order a part, promise a date, or quote coverage. Every
text is a draft you read and send. That is on purpose.
